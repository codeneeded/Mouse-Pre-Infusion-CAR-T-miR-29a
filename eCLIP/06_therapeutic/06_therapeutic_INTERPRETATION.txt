PART A — WHAT WE SAW, AND WHAT IT MEANS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

OVERALL: a coherent memory/persistence candidate module emerges; the
exhaustion-program target set is essentially empty (confirming aim 3); and
the miR-29 / CAR-T link resolves to a clear NEGATIVE that redirects the
CAR-T mechanism rather than confirming it.

1) miR-29 / CAR-T — DEFINITIVE NEGATIVE (the key cross-project result)
   - At RELAXED, single-replicate support, NONE of the 5 CAR-T miR-29a DE
     targets (Dnmt3a, Tet2, Eomes, Tbx21, Ifng) are bound by any miR-29
     family member in any CD8 state. The validation table is empty.
   - What miR-29 DOES bind (311 protein-coding interactions): Camk1d (top,
     78 cluster-rows), Dusp2, E2f8, Btg2, Mcl1, Zfp36, Eif5a, Lmnb1, Top2a -
     a PROLIFERATION / SURVIVAL / signaling set, not epigenetic enzymes.
   Interpretation: the CAR-T miR-29a phenotype is NOT explained by direct
   miR-29 binding of its canonical (3'UTR-predicted) targets in CD8 T cells.
   The effect is likely indirect, stage-specific (outside these 4 states or
   below detection), or routed through the survival/signaling targets above
   (Mcl1, Zfp36, Btg2 are all CD8-relevant). This is a real, publishable
   finding that reorients the CAR-T mechanistic narrative.

2) THERAPEUTIC CANDIDATES — a MEMORY / PERSISTENCE module dominates
   The strongest program signal is memory-survival, where several miRNAs
   converge on the same persistence genes:
   - miR-466i-5p -> Bach2, Bcl2, Il7r, Lef1, Tcf7 (5 memory genes; caveat:
     IP7-inflated, treat as lead)
   - miR-15b-5p / miR-15a-5p / miR-16-5p -> Bcl2, Il7r, Ccr7 (the miR-15/16
     cluster, classic Bcl2 regulators)
   - miR-142a-3p -> Bach2, Bcl2
   - miR-92a-3p -> Klf2 ; miR-210-3p & miR-23a-3p -> Zfp36l2 (memory quiescence)
   Interpretation: a convergent miRNA program targets the memory/persistence
   machinery (Bcl2/Il7r/Ccr7/Tcf7/Lef1/Klf2). For CAR-T persistence, these are
   the miRNAs whose INHIBITION would be predicted to de-repress survival/
   memory genes - the actionable direction for "promote memory/persistence".

3) EFFECTOR AXIS — one clean lead
   - miR-142a-5p -> Id2 3' UTR (memory + exhausted). Id2 drives terminal
     effector differentiation; this is the cleanest canonical effector-axis
     interaction and a candidate for tuning terminal vs memory fate.

4) EXHAUSTION PROGRAM — essentially empty (confirms aim 3)
   Only ONE exhaustion-program interaction: miR-466i-5p -> Cblb (intron, Tn+Tex,
   and miR-466i is the inflated one). No reproducible/relaxed miRNA targeting of
   Pdcd1, Havcr2, Lag3, Tigit, Tox, Nr4a in these data.
   Interpretation: there is no direct miRNA->exhaustion-checkpoint axis to
   exploit here. "Limit exhaustion" is therefore best pursued INDIRECTLY -
   via the memory/persistence module (point 2) that keeps cells out of
   terminal exhaustion - not by a miRNA that directly represses a checkpoint.

5) EXHAUSTION-LEVERAGE CANDIDATES (effector/memory targeters loaded in Tex)
   miRNAs that both target effector/memory programs AND are loaded in exhausted
   cells (so modulating them could push exhausted cells toward function):
   miR-466i (Bach2/Bcl2/Il7r/Lef1/Tcf7; Tex loading high), miR-142a-3p
   (Bach2/Bcl2), miR-466f-3p (Bach2), miR-155-5p (S1pr1), miR-92a-3p (Klf2),
   miR-210/miR-23a (Zfp36l2). These are the top mechanistic hypotheses to test.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
CAVEATS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
   * BINDING != functional repression. Every "candidate" is a hypothesis;
     direction of effect (inhibit vs mimic) depends on functional validation.
   * The single biggest gap for ANY therapeutic claim remains matched
     differentiation-state RNA-seq (target derepression). Recommended next exp.
   * miR-466i naive/Tex inflation (IP7, step-1 QC) - its leads need replication.
   * n=2/state; Tex low capture; relaxed miR-29 search is sensitive but
     single-rep (so its negative for the CAR-T targets is informative).

---------------------------------------------------------------------
PROJECT SYNTHESIS (aims 1-4, all complete at the binding level)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
   Aim 1 (loading): clean miR-150(down)/miR-155(up) gradient across the axis;
     miR-155 dominant in exhaustion; miR-29a engaged throughout.
   Aim 2 (targets): genic targeting shifts naive-intron -> memory-3'UTR; naive
     stemness TFs (Bach2/Lef1/Tcf7) targeted; Id2 3'UTR by miR-142a-5p.
   Aim 3 (dynamics): differentiation adds targeting at the activation switch;
     memory-specific miR-210/miR-23a metabolic+quiescence module (Ndufa4,
     Zfp36l2, Kdm6b); NO exhaustion-specific program.
   Aim 4 (therapeutic): convergent memory/persistence module (Bcl2/Il7r/Ccr7/
     Tcf7/Lef1 via miR-15/16, miR-466i, miR-142a) as the actionable axis;
     miR-29/CAR-T canonical targets NOT directly bound -> CAR-T mechanism
     redirected.
   NEXT EXPERIMENT: matched bulk RNA-seq of the four sorted states to convert
     binding maps into functional (derepression) evidence.

=====================================================================
PART B — MANUSCRIPT RESULTS SNIPPET (aim 4 + miR-29; edit to taste)
=====================================================================

Candidate regulatory miRNAs converge on the memory-persistence program.
Integrating loading, targeting and differential analyses, we nominated
miRNAs with potential to modulate CD8 state. Candidates converged on the
memory/persistence machinery: the miR-15/16 family (miR-15a/15b/16-5p)
together with miR-466i-5p and miR-142a-3p targeted Bcl2, Il7r, Ccr7, Tcf7,
Lef1 and Bach2, while miR-142a-5p targeted the Id2 3' UTR and miR-210-3p
and miR-23a-3p converged on Zfp36l2. In contrast, direct miRNA targeting of
exhaustion checkpoints (Pdcd1, Havcr2, Lag3, Tox) was not observed,
indicating that miRNA control of exhaustion is exerted indirectly through
the memory program rather than via direct checkpoint repression. Finally,
to test the basis of the miR-29a CAR-T phenotype, we asked whether miR-29
family members directly bind their canonical targets in CD8 T cells; even
under relaxed single-replicate criteria, none of Dnmt3a, Tet2, Eomes, Tbx21
or Ifng were recovered as direct miR-29 chimeras. Instead, miR-29 engaged a
proliferation/survival target set (Camk1d, Mcl1, Btg2, Zfp36, E2f8),
indicating that the miR-29a-driven CAR-T phenotype is not mediated by direct
binding of its predicted epigenetic targets in these states.
