 miR-eCLIP CD8 DIFFERENTIATION — STEP 3 / AIM 2 prep INTERPRETATION
 Cluster -> gene/region/biotype annotation (GENCODE vM25, mm10)
 3,396 reproducible chimeric clusters annotated

PART A — WHAT WE SAW, AND WHAT IT MEANS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

OVERALL: every reproducible cluster falls inside an annotated gene, the
step-1 "Other" bucket is now explained, and there is a clear shift in
WHERE miRNAs bind as cells differentiate. Two target findings are credible
and exciting; one (the miR-29 / CAR-T link) is a caution flag to revisit.

1) THE "OTHER" BUCKET IS RESOLVED — and it is GENIC, not intergenic
   - 0 intergenic clusters, 0 antisense clusters. All 3,396 map within genes.
   - The ~30% "Other" from step-1 QC resolves into INTRONS and ncRNA EXONS.
   Interpretation: AGO chimeric targets here are entirely within transcribed
   loci (expected for RISC targeting; cluster calling is gene-model-aware).
   "Other" was never dark matter — it was intronic and ncRNA-exonic targeting
   that the Eclipsebio hierarchy did not break out.

2) TARGETING BECOMES MORE CANONICAL FROM NAIVE -> MEMORY
   Region composition (fraction of clusters):
                3'UTR   5'UTR   CDS    ncRNA_exon  intron
     Naive       15%     1%     4%      23%         57%
     Effector    28%     1%     9%      45%         16%
     Memory      41%     3%    20%      26%         10%
     Exhausted   25%     2%    11%      39%         24%
   Canonical (3'UTR + CDS): Naive 19% -> Effector 37% -> Memory 61% -> Tex 36%.
   Interpretation: naive CD8 targeting is INTRON-dominated (57%), whereas
   memory targeting is predominantly canonical 3'UTR/CDS. As cells acquire
   memory, miRNA regulation shifts toward classical post-transcriptional
   3'UTR control. (Intronic chimeras may reflect co-transcriptional/nascent
   targeting; mechanistically less settled, but a real and testable signal.)

3) TARGET BREADTH AND BIOTYPE
   - Distinct protein-coding targets: Memory 536 > Naive 427 > Effector 208
     > Exhausted 137. Memory has the broadest targetome.
   - Biotype is 63-79% protein_coding across states; other_ncRNA is 15-26%,
     lncRNA 4-8%, miRNA 1-3%.
   Interpretation: the targetome is mostly protein-coding (the biologically
   actionable layer). Exhausted's narrow targetome (137 genes) is partly
   technical (lowest chimeric capture, step-1 QC) - do not over-read it as
   biological target loss without normalization (step 5).

4) NAIVE STEMNESS TFs ARE DIRECTLY TARGETED (credible, aim-3 relevant)
   - Tcf7, Lef1, Bach2 (the naive/stem identity TFs) are all bound in NAIVE,
     predominantly by miR-466i-5p in INTRONS (Bach2 has ~10 reproducible
     clusters; also a 3'UTR site). miR-142a-3p also binds Bach2.
   - Id2 (differentiation TF) is bound in its 3'UTR by miR-142a-5p in MEMORY
     and EXHAUSTED - a clean, canonical regulatory event.
   Interpretation: a coherent hypothesis - in naive cells miRNAs engage the
   stemness-TF network (Tcf7/Lef1/Bach2); Id2 3'UTR targeting appears with
   differentiation. CAVEAT: miR-466i is inflated by the deep IP7 naive
   library (step-1 QC) and its intronic binding is mechanistically uncertain,
   so treat the miR-466i->stemness-TF axis as a lead to validate, not a result.

5) miR-29 / CAR-T CROSS-VALIDATION — CAUTION FLAG
   - miR-29 family has 50 reproducible chimeras, but its top targets are
     ncRNA (Mir5099, a predicted lncRNA) plus protein-coding SINGLES:
     Mcl1, Zfp36 (TTP), E2f8, Top2a, Lmnb1, Camk1d, Hmgcs1.
   - NONE of the CAR-T miR-29a DE targets (Dnmt3a, Tet2, Eomes, Tbx21, Ifng)
     appear as reproducible direct miR-29 chimeras. Tox and Pdcd1 are also
     absent from the reproducible key-gene set.
   Interpretation: the canonical (3'UTR-seed-predicted) miR-29 targets are
   NOT captured as reproducible direct chimeras in these CD8 states. This does
   NOT mean they are unbound - likely they sit below the strict reproducible
   threshold given shallow chimeric coverage (median ~5 reads, step-1 QC).
   ACTION: revisit in step 6 using per-sample (single-rep) clusters and the
   Eclipsebio differential TSVs before concluding absence. The interesting
   POSITIVE is that miR-29 here engages Mcl1 (survival) and Zfp36 (cytokine
   mRNA decay) - plausible CD8-relevant targets worth following.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
CAVEATS / DECISIONS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
   * 0 intergenic/antisense: the resolution code is correct but never needed
     here; if a future dataset has intergenic signal it will be labeled.
   * other_ncRNA (15-26%) likely includes abundant structural RNA (mt-rRNA,
     sno/snRNA) background common to AGO-IP; for the differentiation and
     therapeutic story, restrict to protein_coding targets.
   * Clusters overlapping multiple genes kept the best (protein_coding, then
     widest overlap) - multi-gene loci are simplified.
   * Reproducible-set strictness trades sensitivity for confidence; known
     targets absent here should be checked at per-sample/relaxed support.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO TAKE FORWARD (into step 4, aim 2 networks)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
   - Restrict network/therapeutic analysis to protein_coding targets.
   - Headline leads: miR-466i -> Tcf7/Lef1/Bach2 (naive stemness);
     miR-142a-5p -> Id2 (memory/exhaustion); the naive-intron ->
     memory-3'UTR targeting shift.
   - Carry the miR-29 question to step 6 with relaxed support; do not report
     the CAR-T targets as validated or refuted yet.

=====================================================================
PART B — MANUSCRIPT RESULTS SNIPPET (annotation/targetome; edit to taste)
=====================================================================

Direct miRNA targets are genic and shift toward canonical 3' UTR control
with differentiation.
Annotating reproducible chimeric clusters against GENCODE vM25 assigned
all 3,396 clusters to genic loci; no reproducible interaction mapped to
intergenic or antisense regions. Targets were predominantly protein-coding
(63-79% per state). The genomic distribution of target sites shifted
markedly across differentiation: naive CD8 targeting was intron-dominated
(57% intronic, 15% 3' UTR), whereas memory targeting was predominantly
canonical (41% 3' UTR and 20% CDS; 61% combined), with effector and
exhausted cells intermediate. Memory cells exhibited the broadest targetome
(536 protein-coding genes) and exhausted the narrowest (137), consistent
with their respective chimeric capture depths. Among lineage-defining
transcription factors, the naive/stemness regulators Tcf7, Lef1 and Bach2
were directly targeted in naive cells, predominantly by miR-466i-5p, while
the differentiation regulator Id2 was bound within its 3' UTR by
miR-142a-5p in memory and exhausted cells. Notably, none of the
3'UTR-predicted miR-29 targets (e.g. Dnmt3a, Tet2) were recovered as
reproducible direct chimeras; reproducible miR-29 interactions instead
included Mcl1 and Zfp36.
