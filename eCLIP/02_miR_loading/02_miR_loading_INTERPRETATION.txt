 Per-miRNA ENGAGED ABUNDANCE per state (DESeq2-normalized chimeric coverage)

PART A — WHAT WE SAW, AND WHAT IT MEANS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

OVERALL: the engaged-loading landscape is biologically coherent and tracks
canonical CD8 miRNA biology cleanly. The strongest, most interpretable
signal is an activation/exhaustion gradient carried by two opposing
miRNAs, with additional state-specific engaged miRNAs that make sense.

1) THE CORE FINDING: a miR-150(down) / miR-155(up) gradient
   (values = log2 DESeq2-normalized chimeric coverage, Tn / Teff / Tmem / Tex)
   - miR-150-5p:  8.80 -> 7.21 -> 4.78 -> 2.36   (monotonic DOWN, ~6.4 log2)
   - miR-155-5p:  6.66 -> 9.18 -> 10.45 -> 11.74 (monotonic UP, ~5.1 log2)
   Interpretation: textbook. miR-150 marks naive/quiescent CD8s and is lost
   on activation; miR-155 is activation/exhaustion-driven. miR-155 becomes
   the #1 engaged miRNA in exhausted cells (21.6% of all chimeric signal).
   Two clean opposing monotonic gradients emerging unprompted is strong
   evidence the coverage-based quantification reflects real loading biology.

2) WHAT DOMINATES EACH STATE (within-state % of engaged signal; top hits)
   - Naive:     miR-466i-5p 35.7%, miR-24-3p 12.4%, miR-142a-3p 11.3%,
                miR-150-5p, let-7 family present.
   - Effector:  miR-24-3p 25.1%, miR-466i 12.0%, miR-21a-5p 11.1%, miR-155 3.1%
   - Memory:    miR-24-3p 31.0%, miR-155 8.7%, miR-92a 6.4%, miR-210 2.4%
   - Exhausted: miR-155-5p 21.6%, miR-24-3p 18.8%, miR-466i 9.3%, miR-29a 2.9%
   Interpretation: miR-24-3p is broadly dominant across all ACTIVATED states
   (top in Teff/Tmem/Tex) - a high-abundance engaged miRNA whose targets
   (step 3+) are worth examining. miR-466i dominates naive (caveat below).

3) STATE-SPECIFIC ENGAGED miRNAs (tau specificity, abundance-weighted)
   The most state-restricted miRNAs that are ALSO abundant enough to trust:
   - Effector-specific:  miR-21a-5p (tau 0.92, very abundant) - activation-induced.
   - Memory-specific:    miR-210-3p (0.90), miR-31-5p (0.84), miR-17~92 members.
   - Exhaustion-specific: miR-155-5p (0.82, abundant), miR-29c-3p (0.83).
   - Naive-specific:     miR-150-5p (0.86), miR-181a-5p (0.92), miR-30b-5p,
                         let-7d-3p.
   (Several tau=1 miRNAs exist - miR-222, miR-345, miR-669o - but at very low
   abundance, so treat as noise, not markers.)

4) DIFFERENTIAL LOADING (DESeq2; n=2/state -> EXPLORATORY, but effects large)
   59 miRNAs cleared padj<0.05 & |log2FC|>=1. The dominant axis is ACTIVATION
   (naive vs everything else), with subtler differences AMONG activated states.
   - vs naive (Teff/Tmem/Tex all): strongly INDUCED miR-147-3p (+10-11),
     miR-210-3p (up to +12 in Tmem), miR-182-5p (+9), miR-34a-5p (+6-7),
     miR-21a-5p (+4-8), miR-31-5p (+7-8 in Tmem/Tex), miR-155 (+2.6-3.9);
     LOST: miR-150-5p (down to -4 in Tmem), let-7c.
   - Tex vs Teff: only 2 hits clear threshold - miR-155 UP (+2.76),
     miR-150 DOWN (-3.42). Tex vs Tmem: 0 hits.
   Interpretation: effector/memory/exhausted share a broadly similar engaged-
   miRNA program; the naive->activated switch is the big event, and the
   exhaustion signature on top of it is essentially "more miR-155, less
   miR-150." The very small padj on the large effects (1e-6 to 1e-13) plus
   agreement with known biology make these trustworthy despite n=2.

5) miR-29 FAMILY (direct bridge to the CAR-T miR-29a overexpression project)
   - miR-29a-3p: engaged in ALL states (log2 ~7.7-8.7), highest in Tex (8.75).
   - miR-29c-3p: lower overall but Tex-specific (tau 0.83, peak Tex).
   - miR-29b-3p: stable, modest (~6).
   Interpretation: miR-29a is constitutively loaded across differentiation and
   peaks in exhaustion - so its direct targets (resolved in step 6) are
   relevant in exactly the state the CAR-T work cares about. Good news for
   cross-validating the miR-29a CAR-T phenotype.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
CAVEATS / THINGS TO KEEP IN MIND
---------------------------------------------------------------------
   * miR-466i-5p dominance in naive (35.7%): partly the IP7/IP8 imbalance
     flagged in step-1 QC (IP7 is very deep). miR-466i is a C2MC-cluster
     miRNA of less-established T cell function - do not over-interpret it as
     a naive driver until step-3 targets clarify what it actually binds.
   * tau=1 miRNAs are low-abundance and noise-prone; use the abundance-
     weighted specificity list above.
   * Exhausted has the thinnest engaged layer (step-1 QC), yet miR-155
     dominance there is so large it is robust to that.
   * n=2/state: differential p-values are exploratory. We rely on effect
     size + concordance with biology, not the p-value alone.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO TAKE FORWARD
---------------------------------------------------------------------
   - Priority engaged miRNAs whose TARGETS we most want (step 3): miR-155
     (exhaustion), miR-21a (effector), miR-210 / miR-31 (memory), miR-150 /
     miR-181a (naive), miR-24-3p (broad-dominant), miR-29a/c (CAR-T link).
   - The miR-150->miR-155 switch is the spine of the differentiation story;
     resolving WHAT each binds per state (aims 2-3) is the next question.

PART B — MANUSCRIPT RESULTS SNIPPET (aim 1; edit to taste)
=====================================================================

Engaged microRNA loading reshapes across CD8 differentiation.
To define which microRNAs are actively engaged with targets at each
differentiation state, we quantified chimeric-cluster coverage per
reproducible miRNA and normalized across libraries (DESeq2). Engaged
loading was dominated by a small set of miRNAs whose composition shifted
markedly with differentiation. Naive cells were characterized by high
engaged loading of miR-150-5p, miR-181a-5p and let-7 family members,
whereas activation drove induction of miR-21a-5p, miR-155-5p, miR-147-3p,
miR-182-5p and miR-34a-5p (all log2 fold change > 4 versus naive). Memory
cells showed the strongest engagement of miR-210-3p (log2FC +12.0 vs
naive) and miR-31-5p, while exhausted cells were dominated by miR-155-5p,
which constituted the single most engaged miRNA in that state (21.6% of
chimeric signal). Strikingly, miR-150-5p and miR-155-5p exhibited opposing
monotonic gradients across the naive -> effector -> memory -> exhausted
axis (miR-150 decreasing ~6 log2; miR-155 increasing ~5 log2), and the
distinction between exhausted and effector loading was largely accounted
for by these two miRNAs (miR-155 up, miR-150 down). miR-29a-3p was engaged
across all states and peaked in exhaustion. Because n = 2 per state,
differential loading was treated as exploratory; however, effect sizes
were large and consistent with established CD8 microRNA biology.
