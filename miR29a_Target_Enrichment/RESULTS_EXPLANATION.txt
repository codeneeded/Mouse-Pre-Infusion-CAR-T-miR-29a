===============================================================================
RESULTS EXPLANATION
Mouse Pre-Infusion CAR-T scRNA-seq -- Effect of miR-29a
===============================================================================

This document explains the key findings from the analysis pipeline at the
current state of the project. It is intended for collaborators reading the
repository who want to understand what the data shows without working
through every CSV. Findings are organized by strength of evidence, with
caveats clearly marked.

Last updated: June 05, 2026


===============================================================================
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
===============================================================================

The strongest evidence in the dataset is that miR-29a overexpression is
functionally active in murine pre-infusion CAR-T cells. TargetScan-predicted
miR-29a targets are coherently repressed in both CD4 and CD8 compartments,
the scramble construct controls appropriately for backbone effects, and
canonical miR-29 targets (Tet2, Tet3) appear among the most strongly
repressed genes. This is a robust finding by both pseudobulk DESeq2 and
single-cell MAST, with the expected miRNA-repressor direction asymmetry
(more sig-down than sig-up genes).

Pre-infusion product composition observations (clusters shifting in
abundance between conditions) are visible but still preliminary and require
per-replicate differential abundance testing before they can be reported.


===============================================================================
1. MIR-29A TARGET REPRESSION  [HEADLINE FINDING -- ROBUST]
===============================================================================

QUESTION
--------
Do genes that TargetScan predicts as strong miR-29a targets show stronger
experimental repression in miR-29a CAR-T versus controls?

APPROACH
--------
Two complementary analyses, run on both pseudobulk DESeq2 (primary,
inferentially honest at n=2 biological replicates) and single-cell MAST
(secondary, more sensitive but pseudo-replicated per Squair Nat Comms 2021):

  - Spearman correlation between TargetScan cumulative weighted context++
    score and experimental log2 fold change, across all overlapping genes
    in each (compartment, contrast).
  - Hypergeometric enrichment of significantly down-regulated genes against
    the top-200 strongest predicted miR-29-3p targets and the full conserved
    target list, with significantly up-regulated genes tested as a negative
    control.

PRIMARY CONTRAST (miR-29a vs Scramble), PSEUDOBULK LINEAGE LEVEL
----------------------------------------------------------------

    Lineage     Spearman p   p_BH        Sig-down in top-200   Odds ratio
    ----------  ----------   --------    -------------------   ----------
    CD4         +0.177       1.2e-06     18 obs vs 3.4 exp     5.6x
    CD8         +0.195       3.7e-07     18 obs vs 2.6 exp     7.4x
    Non-T       +0.244       4.3e-05     too few sig genes     (n.s.)
    Innate-like +0.197       1.0e-05     too few sig genes     (n.s.)

SPECIFICITY (EV vs Scramble, negative control)
----------------------------------------------

    Lineage     Spearman p   p_BH         Sig-down in top-200
    ----------  ----------   --------     -------------------
    CD4         -0.062       0.09         0
    CD8         -0.084       0.03         0
    Non-T       -0.067       0.16         0
    Innate-like -0.046       0.30         0

INTERPRETATION
--------------
* miR-29a is functioning as expected. Coherent positive Spearman correlation
  between predicted target strength and observed repression in both major
  lineages, with high statistical significance after BH adjustment.

* The scramble control is doing its job. EV vs Scr gives no target
  enrichment signal -- weak negative Spearman that approximates zero, and
  zero overlap with the top-200 target list. Any miR-29a effect is
  attributable to the miRNA itself, not backbone or insertional effects.

* Effect sizes are biologically meaningful. 5.6-7.4x enrichment of
  significantly repressed genes among the strongest predicted targets is
  a strong signal.

* Canonical targets are recovered. In the Proliferative CD8 effector cluster
  the most strongly repressed validated targets include Tet2, Tet3, Tdg, and
  Atad2b -- all known miR-29 family targets in the DNA methylation /
  hydroxymethylation pathway. Mycn (proliferation TF) is also strongly
  repressed.

CAVEATS
-------
* MAST reports approximately 5x more significant genes than pseudobulk
  (CD4: 2222 vs 415) because it treats each cell as independent. The
  pseudobulk numbers are the inferentially honest ones for the manuscript;
  MAST is concordant in direction and effect-size ranking and should be
  presented as a sensitivity analysis (Squair Nat Comms 2021; Murphy &
  Skene 2022).

WHERE TO LOOK
-------------
  miR29a_Target_Enrichment/Plots/Summary_pseudobulk/1_spearman_forest.png
  miR29a_Target_Enrichment/Plots/Summary_pseudobulk/2_hyper_enrichment_dotplot.png

===============================================================================
2. DIRECTION ASYMMETRY  [SUPPORTING -- ROBUST]
===============================================================================

QUESTION
--------
A miRNA is a repressor, so the transcriptome under miRNA overexpression
should skew toward more sig-down than sig-up genes overall. Does the data
show this miRNA-repressor "fingerprint"?

PSEUDOBULK, LINEAGE LEVEL (miR-29a vs Scramble)
-----------------------------------------------

    Lineage     sig-down    sig-up    down / up ratio
    ----------  --------    ------    ---------------
    CD4          415         221       1.88x
    CD8          303         128       2.37x

CONTROL (EV vs Scramble): roughly balanced or slightly inverted
(CD4: 19 down vs 79 up, CD8: 12 down vs 38 up), as expected for a null.

INTERPRETATION
--------------
The miR-29a transcriptome is clearly down-skewed. The asymmetry is visible
in Plots/Summary_pseudobulk/3_direction_asymmetry.png: miR-29a points sit
well below the y=x diagonal; control points sit on or slightly above it.

This is independent of the target-overlap analysis: it doesn't require any
external target list. It says simply "this perturbation is a repressor at
the transcriptome level," which is what a functional miRNA should do.


===============================================================================
3. WHAT THE FOUR SUMMARY FIGURES SHOW
===============================================================================

Four summary figures are generated per DE method
(miR29a_Target_Enrichment/Plots/Summary_<method>/). Pseudobulk versions are
the manuscript-grade figures; MAST versions are concordant and shown as
sensitivity analyses. Per-figure interpretation below applies to both.


3.1  SPEARMAN FOREST PLOT  (1_spearman_forest.png)
--------------------------------------------------

What it shows: For each compartment (4 lineages + 14 clusters) and each
contrast, a point at the Spearman ρ between TargetScan score and avg log2FC,
with 95% CI from a Fisher z-transform. Point size = -log10 BH-adjusted
p-value.

What to read off it:

  - All miR-29a contrasts (navy primary, mid-blue secondary) sit to the
    RIGHT of ρ = 0 in every compartment.
  - All EV-vs-Scr controls (grey) have CIs crossing zero -- the null
    behaves as a null.
  - The minor T compartments show the weakest miR-29a signal:
      cluster 9  (Treg)                   ρ ≈ 0.08-0.10
      cluster 11 (CX3CR1+ KLRG1+ effector) ρ ≈ 0.05
      cluster 12 (Intermediate activated) ρ ≈ 0.01-0.05
    This is biologically plausible. Treg use different miRNA circuits;
    terminally differentiated CX3CR1+ effectors have less transcriptome
    remaining to remodel; cluster 12's near-null signal is one of several
    flags on this cluster (see Section 5).


3.2  HYPERGEOMETRIC ENRICHMENT DOT PLOT  (2_hyper_enrichment_dotplot.png)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

What it shows: For each (compartment, contrast), the hypergeometric
enrichment of significantly down-regulated genes in the top-200 strongest
predicted miR-29-3p targets. Point size = -log10 p_BH, fill = log2(odds + 1).
Empty cells = too few sig-down genes to test.

What to read off it:

  - The two miR-29a columns are uniformly populated with warm
    (orange-to-red) dots. The EV-vs-Scr column is uniformly pale and
    small -- controls behaving as null.
  - The hottest cells are SMALL compartments:
      Non-T (lineage)         log2(odds+1) ≈ 5+
      Innate-like (lineage)   log2(odds+1) ≈ 4-5
      cluster 8 (Cycling non-T review)
      cluster 13 (NK-like / innate-like review)
    These are REAL effects but driven by tiny denominators -- when a
    compartment only has 15-25 sig-down genes, an overlap of 3-5 with the
    top-200 target list gives a 20-50x odds ratio. Honest signal, but the
    odds-ratio MAGNITUDES should not be reported as the manuscript
    headline.
  - The manuscript-grade effect sizes are the LINEAGE-level CD4 and CD8
    cells: 5.6x and 7.4x odds, p_BH < 10^-8.


3.3  DIRECTION ASYMMETRY  (3_direction_asymmetry.png)
-----------------------------------------------------

What it shows: Log-log scatter of n sig-up vs n sig-down genes per
(compartment, contrast). y = x diagonal as reference. Below the diagonal
= more genes go down than up = repressor signature.

What to read off it:

  - The lineage-level CIRCLES at top-right (large counts) for miR-29a
    contrasts are clearly BELOW the diagonal. Controls sit on or slightly
    above it.
  - The cluster TRIANGLES scatter more (small compartments are noisier)
    but the pattern holds.
  - This is the simplest evidence in the four plots: it requires no
    external target list. It says "miR-29a is a repressor at the
    transcriptome level" using nothing but the DE outputs.

Caveat: this figure currently has a minor truncation of the bottom-right
corner annotation ("more sig-UP than sig-DOWN" gets clipped to "sig-DC").
Cosmetic issue, doesn't affect interpretation.


3.4  CANONICAL TARGET HEATMAP  (4_canonical_targets_heatmap.png)
----------------------------------------------------------------

What it shows: log2FC of 17 canonical or strong-data-driven miR-29 targets
across all 18 compartments, for the primary contrast (miR-29a vs Scr only).
Fill = log2FC clipped at +/-1.5 (blue = repressed, red = upregulated).
Asterisks = significance: * p_adj<0.05, ** p_adj<0.01, *** p_adj<0.001.

This is the most informative single figure for the biological story.
Read by row group:

METHYLATION AXIS (Tet2, Tet3, Dnmt3a, Dnmt3b, Tdg)
  Coordinately repressed across nearly every compartment, with high
  significance. *** in CD4 and CD8 lineages, *** or ** in most clusters.
  This is the cleanest single-pathway result: miR-29a represses the
  entire DNA (de)methylation enzymatic machinery in CAR-T cells.
  Of these, Tdg shows the strongest individual repression in the
  Cycling non-T cluster.

T-BOX EFFECTOR TFS (Tbx21, Eomes)
  EOMES is the single most consistently repressed T-cell-relevant gene
  in the panel -- strongly down (*** significance) in CD4 and CD8
  lineages and across most clusters. This is the mechanistic anchor
  for any "less terminally differentiated / less exhausted" CAR-T
  phenotype: Eomes drives terminal effector / exhausted programs;
  repression of it predicts retention of progenitor-like states.
  Tbx21 is repressed but more variably than Eomes.

MEMORY / QUIESCENCE TFS (Bach2, Foxo3)
  *** NOT uniformly repressed -- state-dependent. ***

  Bach2:
    - Down in CD4 lineage (***).
    - UP in cluster 4 (Non-cycling exhaustion-like CD8)? Light orange.
    - UP in cluster 12 (Intermediate activated CD4) -- *** sig, large red.
    - UP in cluster 13 (NK-like / innate-like review) -- light red.

  Foxo3:
    - Down in CD4 and CD8 lineages (***).
    - UP in cluster 5 (CD4 TCF1hi naive/stem-like progenitor) -- ***
      significance, light red. Notable because Foxo3 is a known marker
      of T-cell quiescence and stemness.

  These reversed-direction findings are biologically interesting:
  Bach2 and Foxo3 are both classical miR-29 targets, so the
  "expected" effect is repression. Two non-exclusive interpretations:

  (a) Indirect upregulation. miR-29a represses a more proximal
      transcriptional repressor of Bach2 / Foxo3, leading to net
      derepression in the cluster contexts where that repressor was
      dominant.

  (b) Cluster-state heterogeneity. Cluster 5 may be a mixture of
      stem-like and non-stem-like cells; miR-29a may be enriching the
      Foxo3-high stem-like subset rather than altering Foxo3 levels
      within individual cells. Per-lineage re-clustering (Section 6)
      may resolve this.

COSTIMULATION (Icos)
  Broadly repressed (*** widely). Note: Icos is not a classical
  miR-29 textbook target -- repression here may be downstream of
  the methylation/TF axis rather than direct.

DATA-DRIVEN HITS (Atad2b, Mycn, Pdgfa, Efna5, Gas7, Dab2ip)
  These were labeled in the per-contrast scatter plots and recovered
  by combined evidence (strong TargetScan score + strong observed
  repression). The heatmap shows they replicate the lineage-level
  story.

  EXCEPTIONS WORTH NOTING:
    - Mycn: deep red (UP) in cluster 12 (Intermediate activated CD4).
      Mycn drives proliferation; if up in this cluster, that may
      contribute to the apparent 5-7x cluster-12 enrichment in the
      composition plots (see Section 5).
    - Dab2ip: deep red (UP) in cluster 11 (CX3CR1+ KLRG1+
      differentiated effector CD4). Another cluster flagged earlier
      for weak overall target enrichment in the forest plot.

WHAT TO TAKE AWAY
  - For the manuscript headline figures: the methylation axis and Eomes
    are the cleanest, most-uniformly-repressed canonical targets. Lead
    the biology section with these.
  - State-dependent Bach2 / Foxo3 behaviour is real and interesting --
    worth a paragraph in the discussion (not a headline) and a note
    that per-lineage re-clustering may clarify it.
  - Cluster 12 (Intermediate activated CD4) accumulates multiple
    anomaly flags: weak target enrichment in the forest plot, Mycn UP
    in the heatmap, Bach2 UP in the heatmap. Cross-reference with the
    apparent 5-7x compositional enrichment of this cluster in miR-29a
    (Section 5). This cluster is the highest priority for per-replicate
    validation before any of these signals make it into a figure.

5. CLUSTER COMPOSITION SIGNALS  [PRELIMINARY -- NOT VALIDATED]
===============================================================================

  * * *  THESE OBSERVATIONS ARE NOT YET STATISTICALLY TESTED  * * *

The cluster-proportion shifts below are visual observations from the
integration composition plots. They require formal per-replicate
differential abundance testing (e.g. propeller from the speckle package)
before they can be reported in the manuscript.

Critically: condition is confounded with capture in this design (one
library per condition per replicate). Cluster shifts must be verified to
occur in BOTH biological replicates and not be driven by a single library.

Visual observations from condition-stratified composition plots:

    Cluster                                  Apparent direction
    ---------------------------------------  -------------------
    12  Intermediate activated CD4           5-7x UP in miR-29a
    8   Cycling non-T (review)               ~7x DOWN in miR-29a
    4   Non-cycling exhaustion-like CD8      ~1.7x DOWN in miR-29a
    5   CD4 TCF1hi naive / stem-like         UP in miR-29a
    0   Activated intermediate CD4           UP in miR-29a
    2   Proliferative CD8 effector           DOWN in miR-29a

If these survive per-replicate testing, the pattern is coherent with the
expected miR-29a biology -- more proliferative and stem-like, less
terminally differentiated, less exhausted -- and matches the wetlab
scientist's qualitative assessment from the marker panels.

Cluster 12 in particular needs attention: a 5-7x enrichment is large
enough that it could be driven by a single library, in which case it
collapses entirely.

7. METHODOLOGICAL DECISIONS (FOR REVIEWERS)
===============================================================================

INTEGRATION
-----------
Harmony was used as the primary integration method, with FastMNN retained
in the same Seurat object as a robustness check. Only abundance- and
cluster-dependent results are method-sensitive; pseudobulk DE is
embedding-independent.

CELL-CYCLE REGRESSION
---------------------
The S-G2M difference (CC.Difference) was regressed during scaling rather
than full S+G2M phase scores. This preserves the cycling-vs-resting axis
of biology while removing the cycling-phase-within-cycling-cells effect
that fragments proliferative clusters. Trade-off: cycling clusters
(0, 1, 7) retain meaningful cell-state structure but their lineage
identity is harder to read because Cd8 drops out in cycling cells -- see
Section 6 item 2 for the planned remediation.

DIFFERENTIAL EXPRESSION
-----------------------
Pseudobulk DESeq2 is the PRIMARY method. With n=2 biological replicates
per condition, pseudobulk is empirical-Bayes-shrunk across approximately
20,000 genes, which makes it tractable at this sample size (Crowell muscat
Nat Comms 2020). MAST run in parallel for cross-method robustness and for
comparability with the OPIS pipeline pattern used elsewhere in the lab,
with the explicit understanding that MAST inflates significance via
pseudo-replication (Squair Nat Comms 2021).

CONTROLS
--------
Scramble (matched backbone, miRNA-sized non-targeting insert) is the
PRIMARY control -- it tests for sequence-specific effects of miR-29a.
Empty vector is a SECONDARY control. Confident hits are significant
versus both controls. EV vs Scr is reported as a QC contrast.

ALL CLUSTERS RETAINED IN DE
---------------------------
Clusters 8 and 13 (wetlab-flagged as review) are tested in DE alongside
the other 12 clusters. The exclude_cluster flag in metadata is wetlab
provenance only and does not drive any subsetting in the inferential
pipeline. Visualizations include all clusters.
